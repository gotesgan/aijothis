## FULL DESCRIPTION

### What it is
Jyotish (hiarya.in) is a mobile-first web app that turns a Vedic birth chart
into a personal AI conversation. Users share their birth date/time/place,
Arya computes their full Kundli with the Swiss Ephemeris (Lagna, Rashi,
Nakshatra, Vimshottari Dasha), then answers their questions about marriage
timing, career, love, money and health — grounded in the real chart and
today's actual planetary transits, never a generic horoscope.

The AI answers honestly (windows, not guarantees), has a built-in crisis
guard for self-harm, and stays consistent across a conversation. It works in
English, Hindi and Marathi with natural Hinglish/Maralish tone.

### Business model
Freemium: free Kundli + 5 free questions → Google signup gate → paid packs.
One-time packs (₹5/₹10/₹20/₹30) + a ₹60/7-day unlimited pass for repeat
buyers. All payments via Razorpay UPI, webhook-verified.

### Why it works
- **Real demand, real payers** — 34 distinct paying users in the first 9 days
- **Repeat revenue signal** — 67% of revenue comes from repeat buyers (one
  user returned 4 consecutive days with zero retention features built)
- **Love/marriage is a massive, high-intent market** — 71% of revenue is
  relationship questions

### Tech stack (complete, clean, TypeScript)
- Next.js 16 (App Router, Turbopack), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind v4
- Swiss Ephemeris WASM (server-side Vedic chart computation)
- Supabase (Postgres, RLS, device-first identity + Google OAuth)
- Razorpay (orders, webhook, idempotent checkout, payment reconciliation)
- Meta Pixel + Conversions API (CAPI, server-side purchase events)
- Microsoft Clarity (session replays/heatmaps)
- next-intl (en/hi/mr), Vercel deployment

### What's already built (beyond the basics)
- **Idempotent checkout** — no duplicate orders on rapid clicks / failed sheets
- **Meta CAPI** — server-side Purchase events, deduped (clean ad signal)
- **Admin control center** — revenue/retention/funnel dashboard (DB-backed auth)
- **Kundli matching** (36-guna Ashtakoota compatibility scoring)
- **Daily Vedic reading** engine (panchang/tithi/nakshatra + transit events)
- **Sachet A/B test** framework — ₹5 trial tier for first-time buyers
- **Cross-device identity** — Google signup links purchases across devices
- **SEO layer** — FAQPage schema, per-language titles, 5+ blog posts, sitemap
- **Safety guard** — crisis/self-harm detection with caring fallback replies
