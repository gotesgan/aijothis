## 4. Patterns and insights

### 4.1 The universal Q1 → Q2 leak (highest priority)
Every user asked **one question, got one answer, then dropped** — before and after the auto-opener removal. The teaser ("Want the exact month?") requires **typing** to continue, and nobody types. **7 of 8 never even saw the signup gate**, so the failure is upstream of pricing.

### 4.2 Engagement is driven by the *problem*, not the UI
Pratiksha went 44 questions because she had a real, urgent problem. Everyone else had a curiosity question. The funnel must manufacture urgency/hook *after* the first answer (a specific open question they can act on), not just offer more reading.

### 4.3 Zero returning users — no re-engagement loop
Across the entire dataset, no device has more than one chat. Nothing brings people back (no push, no "your daily reading is ready", no email). Retention is structurally 0.

### 4.4 Comprehension gap
Pratiksha said it herself: *"Mla nakki kalat ch… tumhi khup confusion kartay"* (I can't clearly understand… you're creating confusion). Dense jargon + contradictory windows confuses users. Fixed direction: plain answers, chart-bound, consistent timing (commits `3208fdf`, `bc3755a`, `ec04a36`).

### 4.5 Language split
Marathi and Hindi users asked richer, more personal questions than the single English user. The Maralish/Hinglish tone is working.

### 4.6 Conversion economics (tiny sample)
1 signup / 8 real users (12.5%), 1 paid / 8 (12.5%). Statistically meaningless without ad traffic — but it proves the paid flow works end-to-end (Razorpay → verification → grant).

## 5. Bugs found & fixed (in the wild)

1. **Empty replies** — Pratiksha got 4 blank answers during rapid-fire questions (17:12–17:13). Cause: empty LLM stream persisted as empty. Fix: silent retry + friendly fallback.
2. **Contradictory timing windows** — Chaman: 2027 vs Aug–Oct 2026. Pratiksha: Feb–Mar 2027 vs Oct 2027–Feb 2028 vs Jan–Feb 2028. Cause: history truncated to 12 messages, so the model couldn't see earlier answers. Fix: window → 30 + prior timing statements injected into the prompt.
3. **Signup gate refresh bypass** — asked-count was in-memory; a refresh reset it, letting Chaman's 3rd question through free. Fix: persist asked-count in localStorage.
4. **Arya suggested deceiving parents** (Pratiksha msg 46: "tell them guna milan < 18/36 even though it's false"). Fix: no-deception rule.
5. **Opener satisfied users** — the auto-overview front-loaded so much value that one typed question felt like enough. Fix: removed the opener.
